# UniqueHub

**A stablecoin-based financial system for saving, sending, and earning yield globally.**

UniqueHub helps users store USDC and cUSD, send money instantly across borders, and earn yield through secure DeFi infrastructure — without complexity.

---

## What UniqueHub does

- **Save** — Store stablecoins in flexible or locked savings vaults designed for real financial goals.
- **Send** — Transfer stablecoins instantly across borders with low fees and fast settlement.
- **Earn** — Earn yield automatically through trusted DeFi protocols like Aave and Moonwell.

## Why UniqueHub

Traditional financial systems are slow, expensive, and limited in global access. UniqueHub provides:

- Stablecoin-based savings
- Instant global transfers
- Transparent on-chain finance
- Automated yield generation
- User-controlled funds

## Security & Transparency

- Non-custodial design options
- Funds held in stablecoins (USDC, cUSD)
- Powered by audited DeFi protocols
- On-chain verifiable transactions

Built on the **Base** and **Celo** ecosystems.
